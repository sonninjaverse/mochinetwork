# Invite your people

Mochi's hosted beta is invite-only. A member can send you a code or a link.

## Join with an invitation

1. Open the invite link, or visit [mochi.meme/gate](https://mochi.meme/gate).
2. Enter the code and choose **Accept invitation**. Each code works once;
   opening a link does not spend it.
3. Choose **Create account** and follow your device's passkey prompt. Your
   account now has three invitations to share. No deposit or transaction is
   needed to receive them.

If a code was already used, ask your friend for another. After accepting it,
finish creating your account in the same browser so it can be connected to
that invitation.

## Invite a friend

Open your profile and choose **Invite friends**. On desktop, the same button
is in the sidebar. You can copy a code or a link with the code filled in.

- **Available:** ready to share with one friend.
- **Accepted:** your friend used the code and can create their account.
- **Joined:** their account is connected; you can open their profile.

The panel shows how many invitations remain and how many friends joined.
Used invitations do not refill when you sign in again. Every new member gets
three invitations of their own.

## Come back with your passkey

Choose **Sign in with passkey** at the invite screen. Use the same passkey you
created when joining; you do not need another invitation. **Sign out** ends the
browser's account session as well as locking the wallet.

Passkeys recover your account. An invitation is for joining, not for recovering
a lost passkey. Keep access to your passkey provider.
